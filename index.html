A mushroom foraging adventure

Blackpine Woods

Start as a backpacker with 5 coins. Examine and identify wild mushrooms, trade with Old Wren, build a home, grow your own, then explore a glowing cave, a misty marsh and a high mountain ridge.

A game, not a field guide. Never eat a wild mushroom based on a description, a picture or a game. Many deadly species look like edible ones. Identification needs an expert.

Mushroom facts are drawn from The Mushroom Bible by Richard Korman, rewritten for the game. Your progress saves in this browser.
